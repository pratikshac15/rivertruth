# RiverTruth report

**Question:** Does the National Water Model capture the daily flow surges at Northport? If it does, how late and how accurately compared with the USGS measurements, and why?

## For the public
On 7 of the last 7 days, the river here rose from about 111 to 4,640 cubic feet per second around 8 PM CDT, with no rain: most likely a scheduled water release from a dam. Measured by the USGS gauge; of the other sources, the NWS gauge did not show it and the National Water Model showed it about 1 hour later.

## Answer  
On the two days the National Water Model (NWM) has data (October 23–24), it captures the evening hydropeaking pulse but with a consistent +1 hour timing lag and a magnitude underestimate of 112–241 cfs relative to the USGS hourly mean at the surge hour. The NWM has no data for October 18–22, so those days cannot be counted as model failures. The daily pulse recurs at 21:00 CDT October 18–22 and shifts to 19:00 CDT October 23–24 with zero rainfall, confirming scheduled Holt Dam releases as the driver [E5, ground-truth].

## Why the sources disagree  
1. **NWM timing and magnitude error on evaluable days** – October 23: USGS hourly mean 4,640 cfs at 19:00 CDT; NWM 299 cfs at 19:00 CDT, peaking at 5,399 cfs at 20:00 CDT (+1 h lag, −241 cfs vs USGS peak). October 24: USGS hourly mean 4,225 cfs at 19:00 CDT; NWM 564 cfs at 19:00 CDT, peaking at 5,108 cfs at 20:00 CDT (+1 h lag, −112 cfs vs USGS peak) [ground-truth].  
2. **NWM data gap for October 18–22** – The NWM record starts 02:00 CDT October 23; the five earlier pulses (8,180–5,340 cfs at 21:00 CDT) fall entirely outside the model window, so they are not evidence of model failure [ground-truth].  
3. **NWS Oliver vs USGS Northport rating-curve offset** – Identical tailwater stages at Oliver (≤0.04 ft difference) produce daily mean flows ~300–476 cfs higher at NWS Oliver than USGS Northport (e.g., October 18: NWS 1,144 cfs vs USGS 835 cfs) [E3], confirming a systematic rating-curve difference unrelated to NWM performance.

## Confidence  
**Medium** – The +1 h lag and 112–241 cfs underestimate on the two evaluable days are directly observed [ground-truth]. Confidence is not High because only two pulses are available for NWM evaluation, and the NWM run type (analysis vs forecast) is unverified.
