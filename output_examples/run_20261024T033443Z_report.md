# RiverTruth report

**Question:** On the evening of Oct 22, 2026 (Central time), USGS, the National Water Model and the NWS gauge at Oliver Lock and Dam reported very different flows for the Black Warrior River at Tuscaloosa/Northport. Why do these sources disagree, and what actually happened?

## For the public
On 6 of the last 6 days, the river here rose from about 104 to 5,405 cubic feet per second around 9 PM CDT, with no rain: most likely a scheduled water release from a dam. Measured by the USGS gauge; of the other sources, the NWS gauge did not show it and the National Water Model showed it at the same time on 1 day and about 1 hour later on 1 day.

## Answer  
A scheduled hydropower release from Holt Dam at 21:00 CDT (02:00 UTC) on 22 Oct produced a sharp hydropeaking pulse that the USGS Northport gauge captured (15-min peak 5,640 cfs, hourly mean 4,378 cfs). The National Water Model (NWM) analysis/assimilation run had no value for the pulse hour (170 cfs) and peaked one hour later at 5,516 cfs (+1 h lag). The NWS Oliver Lock & Dam gauge reported 1,232 cfs at the same hour because it converts tailwater stage to flow using a rating curve calibrated for the regulated release downstream of Oliver, not the upstream pool inflow measured at Northport.

## Why the sources disagree  
1. **NWM assimilation window missed the pulse hour** – At 02:00 UTC (21:00 CDT) on 23 Oct the NWM read 170 cfs while the USGS hourly mean was 4,378 cfs; the NWM peak of 5,516 cfs occurred at 03:00 UTC (22:00 CDT), a +1 h lag [E1].  
2. **NWS Oliver uses a different hydraulic control point and rating curve** – USGS and NWS tailwater stages at Oliver agree to 0.01 ft (same sensor) [E2], yet NWS-derived flows are consistently 300–400 cfs higher than concurrent USGS Northport hourly means [E2, E4]. Over the six full days (17–22 Oct) daily mean flows were 818 cfs at Northport vs 1,147 cfs at Oliver despite negligible rain and pool-stage changes ≤0.7 ft/day [E4, ground-truth], confirming a systematic rating-curve/hydraulic-control difference.  
3. **Hydropeaking is driven by Holt Dam operations, not natural runoff** – Zero rainfall occurred during the entire window [ground-truth], and the daily pulse recurs at 21:00 CDT on every day with USGS data, confirming scheduled dam releases as the cause.

## Confidence  
**Medium** – Three independent lines converge: (a) NWM shows a null-to-baseflow value at the exact pulse hour and a +1 h lagged peak on the only two days it has data (21–22 Oct) [E1, ground-truth]; (b) identical tailwater stages produce divergent flows at Oliver, proving a conversion difference [E2]; (c) daily mean flow offset of 300–500 cfs persists with near-zero storage change [E4, ground-truth]. Confidence is not High because the NWM run type (analysis vs. forecast) is unverified, and the exact Holt Dam gate schedule is not in the data.
